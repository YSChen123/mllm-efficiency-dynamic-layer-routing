# Source attribution

`vendor/AdaLLaVA`, `vendor/LLaVA` and `vendor/lmms-eval` are snapshots of the authors' repositories at the exact commits in `provenance/sources.json`. Their notices and license files are retained. Upstream AdaLLaVA did not include a root license file at the archived revision; this archive does not grant additional redistribution rights. LLaVA and lmms-eval include their upstream licenses. Original wrappers and reports in `reproduction/`, `tools/` and `docs/` describe this reproduction; third-party code remains subject to its original terms.

The vendored lmms-eval snapshot includes the exact executed YAML diff in `provenance/lmms-eval.patch`: public dataset access, pinned dataset revisions and TextVQA OCR enabled. AdaLLaVA and LLaVA tracked source had no modifications. No model weights or datasets are included.
